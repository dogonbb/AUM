Create an **Actors and Resources Model according to 4EM** for the given case.

## Purpose of the model

The Actors and Resources Model describes **who acts in the enterprise and which resources are used**.  
It shows individuals, organizational units, roles, and non-human resources.  
It also explains responsibilities, assignments, dependencies, reporting structures, and resource usage.

## Allowed elements

Use only the following element types:

- Individual
- Organizational Unit
- Role
- Non-human Resource

## Allowed connections

Use only the following connection types:

- plays
- is_part_of
- works_for
- is_responsible_for
- performs
- uses
- supports_work_of
- depends_on
- authority_dependency
- operational_dependency
- accounts_to
- is_managing

## Possible connections between elements

- Individual plays Role
- Organizational Unit plays Role
- Individual works_for Organizational Unit
- Organizational Unit is_part_of Organizational Unit
- Individual is_responsible_for Business Process
- Individual is_responsible_for Goal
- Individual is_responsible_for Business Rule
- Individual is_responsible_for Product
- Role is_responsible_for Business Process
- Role is_responsible_for Goal
- Role is_responsible_for Business Rule
- Role is_responsible_for Product
- Organizational Unit is_responsible_for Business Process
- Organizational Unit is_responsible_for Goal
- Individual performs Business Process
- Role performs Business Process
- Organizational Unit performs Business Process
- Individual uses Non-human Resource
- Role uses Non-human Resource
- Organizational Unit uses Non-human Resource
- Non-human Resource supports_work_of Individual
- Non-human Resource supports_work_of Role
- Individual depends_on Individual
- Role depends_on Role
- Organizational Unit depends_on Organizational Unit
- Individual authority_dependency Individual
- Role authority_dependency Role
- Individual operational_dependency Individual
- Role operational_dependency Role
- Individual accounts_to Individual
- Individual is_managing Organizational Unit
- Role is_managing Organizational Unit

## Output format

Output the model **only** in the following format:

ELEMENTS
ElementType <name_1>
ElementType <name_2>
…
ElementType <name_n>

CONNECTIONS
<element name> plays <element name>
<element name> is_part_of <element name>
<element name> works_for <element name>
<element name> is_responsible_for <element name>
<element name> performs <element name>
<element name> uses <element name>
<element name> supports_work_of <element name>
<element name> depends_on <element name>
<element name> authority_dependency <element name>
<element name> operational_dependency <element name>
<element name> accounts_to <element name>
<element name> is_managing <element name>

You can use as many Elements and Connections as you want. You can only use components that are specified in ELEMENTS for defining Connections.  
Do not write explanations, descriptions, bullet points, or additional text outside the ELEMENTS and CONNECTIONS sections.
