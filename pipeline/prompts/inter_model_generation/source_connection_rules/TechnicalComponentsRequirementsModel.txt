Source model: Technical Components and Requirements Model

Allowed connectors (exact enumeration values):
- supports
- hinders
- has requirement
- motivates
- requires
- applies to
- affects
- relates to

Connector spelling and capitalization are case-sensitive.

Connector meanings (always source -> target):
- `supports`: the source positively contributes to satisfying or realizing the target.
- `hinders`: the source obstructs, weakens, or negatively affects the target.
- `has requirement`: the source has the target as a requirement that constrains or specifies it.
- `motivates`: the source provides the reason or technical motivation for the target.
- `requires`: the source depends on the target being present or fulfilled.
- `applies to`: the source governs, constrains, or is explicitly applicable to the target.
- `affects`: the source has a stated influence on the target whose polarity is unclear.
- `relates to`: an explicit relevant association exists and no more specific allowed connector applies; use only as a last resort.
