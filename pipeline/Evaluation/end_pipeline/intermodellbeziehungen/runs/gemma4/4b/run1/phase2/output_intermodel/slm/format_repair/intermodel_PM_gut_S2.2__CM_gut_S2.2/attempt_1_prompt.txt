## Meaning of internal connections

- A part_of B: Component A is a direct part of Product or Service B.
- A is_a B: Product or Service A is a specialisation, version, variant, or subtype of Product or Service B.
- A requires B: A depends on or needs B in order to work.
- A, B, C PartOF (AND) D: A, B, and C are all parts of D.
- A, B, C PartOF (OR) D: One or more of A, B, and C may be parts of D.
- A, B, C PartOF (XOR) D: Exactly one of A, B, and C may be part of D.
- A, B, C Total-ISA D: A, B, and C are specialisations of D and together form the complete set of variants.
- A, B, C Partial-ISA D: A, B, and C are specialisations of D, but the listed variants are not necessarily complete.

## Structural rules

- Products represent offerings or product variants.
- Services represent explicitly described service offerings.
- Components represent physical or technical parts of Products or Services.
- Features represent functions, capabilities, or measurable customer-relevant characteristics.
- Direct PartOF connections connect Components to Products or Services; PartOF connectors may group Product, Service, Component, and Feature elements.
- Direct ISA connections connect Products and Services; ISA connectors may group Product, Service, Component, and Feature elements.
- Requires represents a dependency, especially a Feature dependency on a Component, Product, or Service.
- AND, OR, and XOR distinguish mandatory, optional, and mutually exclusive compositions.
- Total-ISA and Partial-ISA distinguish complete and incomplete variant sets.
- Connector-based connections may have one or more sources; a single-source connector is valid.
