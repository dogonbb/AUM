Create a Product / Service Model according to 4EM for the given case.

## Purpose of the model

The Product / Service Model describes which products and services the enterprise offers.
It shows products, services, components, and features.
It also explains how products, services, components, and features are composed, specialized, and dependent on each other.

## Allowed element types

Use only the following element types:

- Product
- Service
- Component
- Feature

Do not use any other element types.
Do not create elements such as Goal, Actor, Concept, Technical Component, Market, Constraint, Requirement, or Process.

## Allowed direct connection types

Use only the following direct connection types:

- part_of
- is_a
- requires

## Allowed connector types

Use only the following connector-based connections:

- PartOF (AND)
- PartOF (OR)
- PartOF (XOR)
- Total-ISA
- Partial-ISA

## Meaning of element types

- Product: what the enterprise offers to its customers. A product can be physical, digital, virtual, or service-related and has attributes, benefits, features, functions, or uses that satisfy customer needs or wants.

- Service: a specialization of Product that is offered to customers as a service and is created or delivered as the result of a process.

- Component: a distinguishable part of a product or service that is supplied by a partner or produced within the enterprise.

- Feature: a characteristic or function of a product or service that is valuable to customers or target groups using the product or service.

## Meaning of direct connections

Use direct connections only for simple one-to-one relations.

- A part_of B  
  means that Component A is a direct part of Product or Service B.

- A is_a B  
  means that Product or Service A is a specialization, version, variant, or subtype of Product or Service B.

- A requires B  
  means that Feature A depends on Component, Product, or Service B in order to work.

## Meaning of connector-based connections

Use connector-based connections when one or more elements are connected to one target or when the relation expresses AND, OR, XOR, or ISA grouping. A connector with exactly one source and one target is valid and is required when that class combination is not allowed as a direct `part_of` or `is_a` connection.

- A, B, C PartOF (AND) D  
  means that A, B, and C are all parts of D.

- A, B, C PartOF (OR) D  
  means that one or more of A, B, and C can be parts of D.

- A, B, C PartOF (XOR) D  
  means that exactly one of A, B, and C can be part of D.

- A, B, C Total-ISA D  
  means that A, B, and C are specializations of D and together form the complete set of possible variants.

- A, B, C Partial-ISA D  
  means that A, B, and C are specializations of D, but the list is not necessarily complete.

## Allowed connection patterns

For part_of and PartOF connectors:

- Component part_of Product
- Component part_of Service

For is_a:

- Product is_a Product
- Product is_a Service
- Service is_a Product
- Service is_a Service

For PartOF and ISA connectors:

- Product, Service, Component, and Feature may be connected with Product, Service, Component, and Feature.

For requires:

- Feature requires Component
- Feature requires Product
- Feature requires Service

Do not use requires from Product, Service, or Component unless the dependency is clearly described as a feature dependency and can be modeled through a Feature.

## Modeling rules

1. Use Product for offered products or product variants.
2. Use Service only if the case explicitly describes a service.
3. Use Component for physical or technical parts of a product or service.
4. Use Feature for functions, capabilities, or measurable functions offered by a product or service.
5. Every element used in CONNECTIONS must be listed exactly once in ELEMENTS.
6. Do not invent elements that are not supported by the case text.
7. Prefer connector-based connections for grouped variants, alternatives, or multiple parts.
8. Use Total-ISA when the text gives a complete list of versions or variants.
9. Use Partial-ISA when the text gives only examples or an incomplete list of variants.
10. Use PartOF (XOR) for mutually exclusive alternatives.
11. Use PartOF (OR) for optional or non-exclusive alternatives.
12. Use PartOF (AND) when all listed elements belong together.
13. Do not write explanations, comments, descriptions, bullet points, or any text outside the required output sections.

## Output format

Output the model only in the following format:

ELEMENTS
ElementType <name_1>
ElementType <name_2>
ElementType <name_3>
...

CONNECTIONS
<element name> part_of <element name>
<element name> is_a <element name>
<element name> requires <element name>
<element name_1>, <element name_2>, <element name_3> PartOF (AND) <element name>
<element name_1>, <element name_2>, <element name_3> PartOF (OR) <element name>
<element name_1>, <element name_2>, <element name_3> PartOF (XOR) <element name>
<element name_1>, <element name_2>, <element name_3> Total-ISA <element name>
<element name_1>, <element name_2>, <element name_3> Partial-ISA <element name>


Note that for connections, you shouldn’t include the ElementType in the Connection.
