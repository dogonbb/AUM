Source model: Goal Model

Allowed connectors (exact enumeration values):
- Supports
- Hinders
- Contradicts
- Affects
- Defined by
- motivates
- requires
- uses
- relates to

Connector spelling and capitalization are case-sensitive.

Connector meanings (always source -> target):
- `Supports`: the source positively contributes to achieving or satisfying the target.
- `Hinders`: the source obstructs, weakens, or negatively affects the target.
- `Contradicts`: the source is logically or practically incompatible with the target.
- `Affects`: the source has a stated influence on the target whose polarity is unclear.
- `Defined by`: the source is specified, clarified, or operationally defined by the target.
- `motivates`: the source provides the reason or business motivation for the target.
- `requires`: achieving or addressing the source depends on the target being present or fulfilled.
- `uses`: the source explicitly uses the target as a means, input, or resource.
- `relates to`: an explicit relevant association exists and no more specific allowed connector applies; use only as a last resort.
