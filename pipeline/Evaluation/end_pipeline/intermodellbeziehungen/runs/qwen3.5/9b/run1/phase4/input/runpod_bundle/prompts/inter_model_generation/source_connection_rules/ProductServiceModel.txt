Source model: Product-Service-Model

Allowed connectors (exact enumeration values):
- motivates
- requires
- creates
- relates to
- responsible for

Connector spelling and capitalization are case-sensitive.

Connector meanings (always source -> target):
- `motivates`: the source provides the reason or demand for the target.
- `requires`: the source depends on the target for its provision, realization, or operation.
- `creates`: the source produces or brings the target into existence.
- `relates to`: an explicit relevant association exists and no more specific allowed connector applies; use only as a last resort.
- `responsible for`: the source is explicitly responsible for the target in the sense stated by the case.
