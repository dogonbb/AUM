Create a **Business Rules Model according to 4EM** for the given case.

## Purpose of the model

The Business Rules Model describes **which rules, conditions, and restrictions apply in the enterprise**.  
It shows rules that control processes, influence decisions, support goals, or restrict behavior.  
It also explains how rules are related to goals, processes, concepts, and other rules.

## Allowed elements

Use only the following element types:

- Business Rule
- Derivation Rule
- Event-Action Rule
- Constraint Rule
- Static Constraint
- Transition Constraint

## Allowed connections

Use only the following connection types:

- supports
- conflicts
- hinders
- triggers
- controls
- AND_decomposition
- OR_decomposition
- refers_to

## Possible connections between elements

- Business Rule supports Goal
- Business Rule supports Business Process
- Business Rule supports Business Rule
- Business Rule conflicts Goal
- Business Rule conflicts Business Rule
- Business Rule hinders Goal
- Business Rule hinders Business Process
- Event-Action Rule triggers Business Process
- Constraint Rule controls Business Process
- Static Constraint controls Concept
- Transition Constraint controls Business Process
- Business Rule AND_decomposition Business Rule
- Business Rule OR_decomposition Business Rule
- Business Rule refers_to Concept
- Derivation Rule refers_to Concept
- Constraint Rule refers_to Concept

## Output format

Output the model **only** in the following format:

ELEMENTS
ElementType <name_1>
ElementType <name_2>
…
ElementType <name_n>

CONNECTIONS
<element name> supports <element name>
<element name> conflicts <element name>
<element name> hinders <element name>
<element name> triggers <element name>
<element name> controls <element name>
<element name_1> <element name_2> <element name_3> … <element name_n> AND_decomposition <element name>
<element name_1> <element name_2> <element name_3> … <element name_n> OR_decomposition <element name>
<element name> refers_to <element name>

You can use as many Elements and Connections as you want. You can only use components that are specified in ELEMENTS for defining Connections.  
Do not write explanations, descriptions, bullet points, or additional text outside the ELEMENTS and CONNECTIONS sections.
